# Start your content engine

Your first welcome offers **1. Curated interview** or **2. Mega file dump**.
Choose a few tailored questions, bring a folder of real source material, or
combine both. File-derived profile suggestions are yours to confirm or correct.

Open this folder in your preferred AI agent and say:

> Kick off my content engine. Walk me through it, ask a few questions at a time, and turn my real work into my first batch of content.

The agent will learn who you help and what you want to achieve, find useful material in your work, learn how you sound, and create a small batch for your review. If you have no documents ready, a conversation about a recent project or customer question is enough to start.

You leave with your Brand Brain, Voice, content pillars, offer, sourced idea bank, first drafts and a first-week launch plan. Your data stays in this folder and setup can resume later.

**Instructions for the AI agent:** read `.contentmaxxing/skills/kickoff/SKILL.md`. If this is the source repository, read `contentmaxxing/skills/kickoff/SKILL.md` instead. Run the guided kickoff; handle commands, files and task results yourself. Use `contentmaxxing start` to initialize or resume the creator workspace. Ask the creator for their context and material, then keep working through the first batch. Content remains subject to creator review before publication.
