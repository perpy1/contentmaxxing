# Curated interview

Use `start --mode interview`. Ask at most three related questions at once, and
reuse facts already supplied in the conversation, profile draft, or sources.
Follow the creator's answers; this is an interview, not a required questionnaire.

Start with the most useful gaps:

- **Direction:** What do you do, who do you help, and what should content do for you?
- **Positioning:** What do people already ask you for? What do you disagree with
  in your field? What do you want to become associated with?
- **Offer:** What do you sell, if anything, and who gets the most value from it?
- **Evidence:** Walk me through a recent project, decision, mistake, or win.
  What did you actually do? What changed? What evidence can we show?
- **Voice:** Share something that sounds like you. What language or style would
  feel fake coming from you? Treat stated preferences separately from observed voice.
- **Boundaries and pace:** What should stay private or off limits? Which platform
  should we start with, and what weekly pace is sustainable?

Choose the next questions from the answers and gaps, rather than asking every
question in order. Save partial facts using `start --answers <file>`. Optional
unknowns can remain empty; the creator can skip them and refine later. Preserve
their exact descriptions of real work as source material for the first batch.

If the creator already has files, offer to switch or combine with the file-dump
path. Never restart their profile just because the input method changes.
