# Review checklist

- Topic, format and primary job are distinct and intentional.
- Specific source evidence supports experiences, numerical claims and proof. Quotes match stored line references. Distinguish creator speech from another participant's experience.
- Voice comes from actual samples; no automatic lowercase, slang or Gabe identity.
- One proof object per major article claim; certainty has scope fences.
- Keep evidence-audit and production notes in `quality_notes`. In the public body, retain a qualification when it changes the claim or how the reader should use it; do not append an unmeasured-results disclaimer to a routine observation that makes no outcome claim. Missing proof still limits what you can say, and material boundaries must remain visible.
- Low polish + high specificity means real receipts and working notes, not deliberately poor design.
- The platform expression is native. Does an X draft look like unchanged LinkedIn prose? Review its generic polish.
- A smart 22-year-old could screenshot an actionable section and use it this afternoon.
- No generic advice, manufactured vulnerability, fake profound endings, repeated stems or copy-paste derivatives.
- CTA only when it serves the job and after delivered value. Some X posts can be funny, weird, blunt or observational without a lesson.
- Check restrictions, source consent and redaction before approving. A reference proves provenance, not the truth of every generated interpretation.
